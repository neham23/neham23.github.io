Your 7-day at-home routine, organized and ready to go.

This Week at a Glance

Each workout is designed to be 20–30 minutes long, using minimal or no equipment. Choose the level that feels right for you and focus on consistency over perfection.

How to Use This Plan

  • Pick a time of day that you can stick to most days.
  • Warm up for 3–5 minutes before each session.
  • Move at your own pace and modify any exercise as needed.
  • Check off each day as you complete it to stay motivated.
