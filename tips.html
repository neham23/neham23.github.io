Tips & Tricks

Helpful advice to keep you consistent, confident, and injury-free.

Warm-Up Essentials

A good warm-up prepares your body for movement and helps prevent injuries.

  • Arm circles – 30 seconds
  • March in place – 1 minute
  • Light stretching – 2 minutes

Stretching Tips

  • Hold each stretch for 10–20 seconds.
  • Avoid bouncing — move slowly and gently.
  • Breathe deeply to relax muscles.

Watch these short clips for basic squat and plank form.

How to Stay Consistent

  • Choose a workout time you can keep every day.
  • Lay out your workout clothes the night before.
  • Start small — 10 minutes is better than nothing.
  • Celebrate every completed workout.
